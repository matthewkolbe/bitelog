# Bite Log Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 30, 2026*

Bite Log has no server and no accounts. The developer does not collect,
receive, or store any of your data.

## What stays on your phone

- Your food log, goals, custom foods and recipes.
- Photos you take in the app and your voice recordings.
- Your model settings. API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Voice is turned into text on your phone by Apple's speech recognition.
Recordings are not sent anywhere.

This data is included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, if you
use them, like any other app's data. Deleting the app deletes it from the
phone.

## What goes to your AI model provider

Bite Log works with an AI model you choose and connect yourself, such as
Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a server you run. When you log a
meal, change an entry, add a product or build a recipe, the app sends that
provider:

- the text of what you ate (typed, or transcribed on your phone),
- photos you chose to have read (your plate, a package label, a recipe page),
- the matching entries from the app's built-in USDA food database,
- your API key, to sign in.

The provider handles this data under its own privacy policy and terms.
Bite Log sends nothing to any other party.

## What Bite Log does not do

- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No account, sign-in, or data sharing with the developer.
- No location, contacts, or health data.

## Permissions

- **Microphone and speech recognition:** to record and transcribe what you
  say you ate, only while you hold the mic button.
- **Camera:** to photograph food, labels and recipes.
- **Local network:** only if you connect a model server on your own network.

## Children

Bite Log is not directed at children under 13.
