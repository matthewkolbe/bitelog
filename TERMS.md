## Numbers are estimates

Calories and nutrients are estimates. They come from the USDA FoodData
Central database and from an AI model's reading of what you said, typed, or
photographed. The AI can misunderstand, misjudge amounts, or pick the wrong
food, and the database values are averages that won't exactly match what you
ate. Check anything that matters to you.

stop using it and reach out to a doctor or an eating disorder helpline. In
the U.S., the ANAD helpline is 1-888-375-7767.
